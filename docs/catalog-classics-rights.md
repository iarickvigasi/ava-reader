## Evidence and result

Every exported seed includes title, language, full author name, known catalog name variants, gender,
author death year, and a bibliography URL. Bibliographies link to Wikisource's texts/scans and author
records, or Wolne Lektury's own Polish catalog and biographies. These sources are discovery and
original-text evidence; they do not license an arbitrary Open Library/Internet Archive edition.
Titles include novels, plays, stories, poems, and collections; aliases do not count as extra works.
A collection and a separately listed constituent work can overlap; these are 300 discovery targets,
not 300 verified standalone EPUBs. The added titles use the same linked author bibliographies.
The two Moby-Dick spellings now share one seed. Eight Russian entries became eight Ukrainian works.
Conan Doyle (died 1930) was replaced with Edgar Allan Poe's The Gold-Bug: he fails this list's
conservative life-plus-100 screen, despite his original texts being public domain in the EU.

All selected original texts were published before 1931; all listed authors died by 1924. Their
ordinary life-plus-70 and life-plus-100 economic terms have elapsed by this review date. This makes
them suitable original-text candidates for Europe and broad international distribution, subject to
edition-specific verification. It does not establish that Open Library has an unrestricted EPUB.

## Legal basis and limits

- [EU Directive 2006/116/EC](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX%3A32006L0116),
  Article 1: generally life plus 70 years; Article 8 counts from the following January 1.
  Article 10 preserves applicable longer existing terms; this is not a universal national-law audit.
- [Mexico's copyright law, Article 29](https://www.wipo.int/wipolex/en/legislation/details/23966)
  supplies a longer life-plus-100 comparison. Passing it is a screening rule, not worldwide clearance.
- [US Copyright Office: duration](https://www.copyright.gov/circs/circ15a.pdf) describes the
  publication-based rules. Original texts published before 1931 have passed the ordinary 95-year
  published-work ceiling in 2026; a recent first publication or new edition needs separate review.
- [Berne Convention, Articles 2 and 7](https://www.wipo.int/wipolex/en/text/283698) separately
  protects translations/adaptations and permits national differences in duration.

Before Publish, verify the exact file's text, translator/coauthors, illustrator, introduction,
annotations, first publication, source terms, and evidence for the intended distribution territories.
A modern translation or illustrated edition can remain protected after the original text expires.
National exceptions, moral rights, and public-domain remuneration rules prevent a blanket promise of
free worldwide reuse. A public reading link or an IA publicdomain flag alone is insufficient.
The current application records territories but does not geo-enforce them; do not approve a
territory-limited file for an unrestricted audience. Existing admin review remains mandatory.
