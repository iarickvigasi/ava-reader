# Reader · pagination

## Summary
Lays rendered content into discrete pages/columns and decides which page is shown, including after a
navigation or resume request.

## Scope
- In: column/page layout, geometric measurement of content, splitting a chapter into pages, choosing
  the current page, reflow on resize/font change, honouring a pending restore intent.
- Non-goals: rendering blocks (see content-rendering), choosing the target position (see
  navigation/resume).

## Behaviour
1. Content is measured (measurement/) to compute page boundaries for the current viewport and font
   scale.
2. resolvePaginationDecision picks currentPageIndex from inputs (active chapter, restore intent,
   page resolution: exact | block-start).
3. A pending RestoreIntent pins the page to a block/edge until consumed. A sticky edge intent stays
   pinned across relayout only while the reader is still on that edge; once they page away, the pin
   releases so later relayouts preserve their current position (visible locator) instead of snapping
   back to the edge (shouldKeepStickyRestorePinned).
4. Resize or font-scale change recomputes the layout key (createPaginationLayoutKey) and
   re-paginates, keeping the reader near the same block.

## Data & sync
Pure functions over measurement results + RestoreIntent (from navigation.ts).
PaginationDecisionInput → PaginationDecision. No I/O.

## Edge cases
Measurement failure (status failed) → fallback to block-start; viewport mid-resize; font change
mid-read; chapter shorter than one page; restore intent referencing a missing block; a mobile
viewport change (address-bar show/hide) that re-measures mid-chapter must not snap a reader who has
paged away back to the chapter edge.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Resizing or changing font re-paginates and keeps the reader on the same block.
- [ ] A restore intent lands on the correct page, then is consumed.
- [ ] Measurement failure degrades to block-start without crashing.
- [ ] Once the reader pages away from a chapter edge, a relayout/re-measure keeps them on their
      current page — it never jumps back to the first/last page.

## Open questions
Scroll vs paged mode toggle; two-page spread rules on wide screens.
